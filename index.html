About in.house

An old island house,
slowed down for divers.

in.house is a renovated old home on Xiaoliuqiu, a small coral island off Taiwan's southwest coast. Terrazzo floors, potted greens, a wall that says "put down your phone and talk to the people around you" — and a resident cat who takes the lobby shift.

  • A freediving-friendly stay: gear-drying racks, rinse space, weights and buoys at home here.
  • Self check-in — book online, verify your booking, get your door code. No front-desk queue.
  • Five rooms, one old house: doubles, twins, a single, and a bunk dorm.

Getting Here

A small island, 25 minutes from Donggang

  • Take the ferry from Donggang Harbor (Pingtung) — about 25–30 minutes to Xiaoliuqiu.
  • On the island, a scooter or electric bike is the easiest way around — rentals cluster near the harbor.
  • The exact address and a walking map are included in your booking confirmation.
